  How can the stationary distributions and standard performance measures of

  FCFS discipline throughout. Markovian models: M/M/r and M/M/1+M queues with a

method: >
  For the CTMC models, partial balance equations are solved to give the
  stationary law as an integral representation plus a terminating generalized
  hypergeometric sum, the two pieces obeying the same recursion with different
  boundary conditions. For GI/GI/1 and GI/GI/r, modified Lindley and
  Kiefer-Wolfowitz recursions are introduced and written in operator form, from
  which the stationary distribution is expressed as a convergent series solving a
  modified Wiener-Hopf equation. For GI/GI/infinity, an adapted Kiefer-Wolfowitz
  recursion on the vector of elapsed service times is used.

  even zero service rate; a unified operator/series characterization for GI/GI/1
  and GI/GI/r with resetting that holds whether or not the underlying queue is
  stable; and an explicit stationary law for GI/GI/infinity with resetting.

# Queueing models with random resetting

Explicit stationary distributions and performance measures for M/M/r, M/M/1+M and
GI/GI/* queues that reset to empty at random times. Markovian cases solved via
partial balance equations; non-Markovian cases via modified Lindley and
Kiefer-Wolfowitz recursions in operator form solving modified Wiener-Hopf
equations. Resetting makes the queue positive recurrent for any traffic intensity.
