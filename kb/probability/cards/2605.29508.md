# Quantum Markovian Dynamics from a Double Covariance Stochastic Framework

Derives the Lindblad (GKSL) master equation from local Ito SDEs on subsystem
Hilbert spaces by multi-scale Ito calculus and sliding-window averaging in a
hydrodynamic limit: deterministic drift gives the Hamiltonian part, quadratic
noise covariance gives the dissipative part, and the noise-free limit is the von
Neumann equation.
