# Multi-Dimensional Opinion Formation

A multi-topic opinion-dynamics model where agents carry importance weights and the
update on one topic depends on weighted similarity across the full opinion vector.
A kinetic equation is stated and its mean-field PDE derived; importance weights
are shown to control the stationary structure and can make the mean opinion drift
and the variance grow.
