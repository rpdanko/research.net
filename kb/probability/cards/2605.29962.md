# Gaussian Multiplicative Chaos for i.i.d. matrices

Proves that normalized powers |det(X - z)|^gamma of the characteristic polynomial
of a non-Hermitian i.i.d. matrix converge to Gaussian Multiplicative Chaos on the
disc across the full subcritical range gamma in (0, 2 sqrt 2), for both symmetry
classes. First such result for a non-invariant ensemble; proof uses Dyson
Brownian motion, the matrix Dyson equation, and K-point function asymptotics.
