# Spectral Independence and Local-to-Global Techniques for Optimal Mixing of Markov Chains

Monograph-length unified treatment of spectral independence (a spectral bound on
the pairwise influence matrix) and local-to-global theorems, showing they imply
optimal mixing of Glauber dynamics and the matroid bases-exchange walk. Proofs are
given from a spectral-gap / functional-analysis viewpoint, including the
Trickle-Down Theorem.
