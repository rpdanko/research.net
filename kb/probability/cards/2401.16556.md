# Duality of causal distributionally robust optimization

Dynamic duality formulas for distributionally robust optimization where the
ambiguity set is a causal / adapted Wasserstein ball. The worst-case problem
becomes inf over a multiplier of a conjugate penalty plus a dynamic-programming
value, computed recursively in discrete time and by a path-dependent HJB equation
in continuous time. Extended to expected shortfall and to optimal stopping.
