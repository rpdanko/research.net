  roughness of the noise, in the spirit of the Hairer-Ryser-Weber triviality
  results for the 2D Allen-Cahn equation.

# Stabilization by rough noise for an epitaxial growth model

Adding arbitrarily small space-time white noise to a Schwoebel-barrier epitaxial
growth SPDE is shown to kill all nonlinear interaction in the vanishing-regularization
limit, collapsing the equation to its stable linear part and suppressing hill
growth. Proof via an explicit Fourier-series Da Prato-Debussche computation.
