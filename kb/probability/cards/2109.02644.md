# Resolvent convergence for sample second-moment matrices with heterogeneous profile

Quantitative deterministic equivalents for the resolvent of (1/n) X X^T with
independent, non-identically-distributed, possibly non-centered columns having
arbitrary within-column dependence. Assumptions enter only through moments of
centered quadratic forms; no sub-Gaussianity, global concentration, entrywise
independence, or limiting profile is required. Fixed z only.
