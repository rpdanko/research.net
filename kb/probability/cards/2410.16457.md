# The circular law for random band matrices with arbitrary doubly stochastic variance profile

Establishes the circular law for non-Hermitian random band matrices with power-law
bandwidth n^gamma and only a doubly stochastic variance profile, improving the
known threshold from gamma > 32/33 to gamma > 5/6 (Gaussian) / gamma > 8/9
(subgaussian). The new technical input is a profile-independent polynomial lower
bound on intermediate small singular values.
