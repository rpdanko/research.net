# Inertial viscosity Tseng extragradient for pseudomonotone variational inequalities

An inertial, viscosity-anchored version of Tseng's forward-backward-forward method
for variational inequalities with a pseudomonotone Lipschitz operator in Hilbert
space. A self-adaptive step size removes the need for the Lipschitz constant, only
one projection is computed per iteration, and the viscosity term forces strong
convergence. Applied to variational inequalities from optimal control. Methods and
results sections were not available; card at low confidence.
