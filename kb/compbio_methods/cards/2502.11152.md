# Error bound for the regularized loss of deep linear networks

Local geometry of the Frobenius-regularized squared loss of an L-layer linear
network. Gives a closed form for the full critical-point set (any depth, not just
global minima) and proves a local error bound there: distance to the critical set
is bounded by the gradient norm, under mild width and regularization conditions.
This implies linear convergence of gradient descent to a critical point, confirmed
experimentally. Conclusion section unavailable; card at low confidence.
