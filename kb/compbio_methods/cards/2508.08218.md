# Distributed asynchronous generalized momentum without delay bounds

A distributed generalized-momentum algorithm (subsuming heavy ball and Nesterov)
that converges under total asynchrony with arbitrary finite delays and no delay
bound. The one-step momentum map is not a max-norm contraction, so the analysis
shows the two-step composed map is one and applies classical asynchronous
contraction theory, under a diagonal-dominance condition on the Hessian. Gives
parameter conditions and a linear rate in operation cycles; simulations report
16-36% fewer iterations than GD, heavy ball, and Nesterov under unbounded delays.
