  Synthesizing an arbitrary unitary as a quantum circuit from a parameterized gate

# Gradient descent for depth- and gate-optimal unitary synthesis

Empirical study: gradient descent (GRAPE-style) reliably finds depth-, entangling-,
and total-gate-optimal circuits for generic unitaries without overparameterization,
including under 1D-chain and star connectivity, provided the circuit skeleton is
chosen by a prescription that avoids parameter-deficient topologies. Diagnoses
prior random-search failures as inadvertent selection of skeletons whose layer
symmetries collapse the effective parameter count. Findings are empirical, not proven.
