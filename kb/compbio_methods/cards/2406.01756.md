# Polynomial-hierarchy completeness of fortification-interdiction games

Reductions placing standard tri-level Defender-Attacker-Defender problems at exact
levels of the polynomial hierarchy: Sigma^p_2 for max-flow and shortest-path
interdiction with fortification and for an electric-grid defence problem, Sigma^p_3
for the unit-weight multi-level critical-node problem, and completeness for every
level for a multi-round interdiction knapsack that then serves as a base problem
for future hardness proofs. Mostly unit-cost regime; results are worst-case.
