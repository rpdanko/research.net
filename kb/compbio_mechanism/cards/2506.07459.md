# ProteinZero: online RL for self-improving inverse folding

Treats a pretrained inverse-folding model as a policy and fine-tunes it online on
its own generations, with cheap surrogate rewards (ESMFold refolding for
designability, a self-derived likelihood-ratio ddG for stability), a KL anchor to
the pretrained model, and an embedding-space diversity regularizer against mode
collapse. Sweeps RL algorithms (GRPO, RAFT, DPO). Reports 36-48% failure-rate
reductions on CATH-4.3 versus ProteinMPNN / ESM-IF / InstructPLM with held-out
oracle checks, in a three-day single-node run. The stability reward is a
conditional-over-marginal log-likelihood ratio — pointwise mutual information
between sequence and structure — under a biological name. All validation is in
silico; the Discussion section was not in the extracted text.
