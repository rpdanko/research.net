# Probing and steering biology across Boltz-1's trunk-diffusion boundary

Uses linear probes, sparse autoencoders and causal interventions on Boltz-1's
Pairformer trunk and diffusion module. The trunk keeps geometry and sequence
chemistry both linearly decodable; the diffusion module keeps geometry but
attenuates chemistry. Steering the trunk's single representation along
probe-derived directions induces helix and coil dose-dependently but not
beta-strand (F1 = 0.82 yet no causal effect) — decodability and causal
steerability are distinct. Scoring against sparse SwissProt annotations inflates
false positives, so interpretability scores are lower bounds; supervised probes
beat single SAE features wherever a label exists. The missing-label issue is a
positive-unlabeled evaluation problem stated in biological terms.
