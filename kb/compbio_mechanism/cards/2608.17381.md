  best-of-N sampling, and improvements transfer to held-out predictors

  feasibility signals into the same decision loop rather than as post-hoc
  filtering, and supports multiple folding experts through
  consensus/disagreement signals.

# MCTH: Monte Carlo Tree Hallucination for biomolecular co-design

Inference-only sequence-structure co-design. Frozen folding (all-atom diffusion)
and inverse-folding models are black-box operators proposing transitions between
hallucinated sequence-structure states; Monte Carlo Tree Search allocates a fixed
folding budget across competing design trajectories, with value combining model
confidence, uncertainty, cross-expert consensus/disagreement, and optional
biophysical feasibility terms. One shared planning layer across protein-RNA,
protein-DNA, protein-protein and protein-ligand design. Beats fixed cycling and
best-of-N under matched budget; gains transfer to held-out AlphaFold3 / Chai-1.
The selection rule is UCT and the multi-expert signal is query-by-committee,
neither named as such. All validation is computational.
